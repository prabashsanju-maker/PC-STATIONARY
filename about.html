Our Story

Crafting History.

PC Stationery was born from a simple belief: The tools we use to create should be as inspiring as the ideas they bring to life. Established in 2020 in the heart of Sri Lanka, we began as a small boutique dedicated to sourcing the world's most elegant writing instruments.

Our Mission

To bridge the gap between traditional craftsmanship and modern productivity by providing stationery that transcends utility and becomes a companion for life's greatest moments.

Our Vision

To become the premier destination for stationery enthusiasts in Sri Lanka, fostering a community that values the tactile beauty of paper and ink in a digital world.

Over the years, we have expanded our collection to include everything from luxury fountain pens to eco-friendly archival journals and minimalist workspace organizers. Every item in our store undergoes a rigorous selection process to ensure it meets our standards of durability, aesthetic appeal, and functional excellence.

Beyond the Desk.

At PC Stationery, it's not just about selling products; it's about the feeling of a nib moving across smooth parchment, the smell of fresh linen covers, and the confidence that comes from using a well-crafted tool.

"Writing is the geometry of the soul. We simply provide the tools to build it."
